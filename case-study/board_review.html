In one line

The company wins customers but loses too many. ARR grew 26% to $8.4M, but it kept only 56% of its starting ARR in Y5, still loses about $10M a year, and has about 13 months of cash. The Y6 renewals will decide what comes next.

    Trends, drivers, concerns

    A. Growth: ARR is up, revenue is not

    Contracted ARR vs revenue run-rate

    Revenue run-rate = quarterly revenue x 4.

    • Trend: ARR rose 26% in Y5; revenue fell 2.5% (audited).
    • Concern: the gap means signed ARR is not all turning into revenue. Only 79% did in Y5.

    Revenue by business line

    Quarterly revenue.

    • Driver: Business Line 1 fell 52% from its Y3 Q4 peak.
    • Driver: Business Line 2 is now 81% of revenue but grew only 12% in Y5.

    B. Retention: the main problem

    ARR won and lost each quarter

    Above zero: new and upsell ARR. Below zero: ARR lost.

    • Trend: losses grew from $1.4M (Y3) to $3.0M (Y5) a year.
    • Concern: Y5 Q1 alone lost $1.4M, half of the year's losses.

    Share of ARR kept, on a yearly basis

    Each quarter's losses scaled to a full year. Line = 85%, the healthy level.

    • Trend: below 80% in every quarter since Y3 Q2.
    • Positive: Y5 Q2 to Q4 recovered to 69-78% after the Y5 Q1 low of 39%.

    C. Sales pattern and unit economics

    Y5 new and lost ARR by month

    Darker bars are the last month of each quarter.

    • Driver: 72% of Y5 new ARR was signed in the last month of a quarter.
    • Concern: losses peak in month 1 and month 12, a year after big signing months. The big M6 and M9 deals ($2.1M) renew in Y6.

    Cost to win a customer vs first-year gross profit

    Per new customer. Cost uses full sales and marketing spend.

    • Concern: winning a customer ($97k) costs almost twice its first-year gross profit ($51k). Customers stay about 27 months, so they barely pay back.
    • Positive: deal sizes are growing: $58k (Y3) to $67k (Y5).

    D. Burn and cash

    Quarterly EBITDA

    Loss before interest, tax and depreciation. Y4 Q4 corrected.

    • Trend: losses peaked at $4.0M in Y3 Q3 and fell 21% in Y5.
    • Driver: people cost fell 31%, but rent, G&A and other costs rose about 20%.

    Burn multiple

    Loss for each $1 of ARR added. Line = 2x, the healthy level.

    • Trend: 17x in Y4, 5.9x in Y5. Growth costs three times what it should.

    Where the cash went in Y5

    From $19.1M to $11.7M. USD.

    • Concern: $3.1M of customer prepayments softened the loss. Without them, cash lasts about 13 months, not 19.
    • Concern: $1.5M moved into non-current assets and $1.1M left cash with no explanation in the P&L.
